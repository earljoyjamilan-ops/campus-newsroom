Sample News Mock-up

A model campus-news article students can read to understand the parts of a complete news story. This is a sample for training, not a report of an actual school event.

CategoryNews

Students Join Campus Clean-Up Drive to Promote Environmental Responsibility

Headline: Clear, specific, and tells the main news event.

BylineBy Maria Santos, Student Writer | Published: July 14, 2026

Sample Event Photo Area

CaptionGrade 8 students collect recyclable materials during a campus clean-up activity at the school grounds. Photo by The Living Stone staff.

LeadMore than 120 students participated in a campus clean-up drive at The Living Stone school grounds on Friday to encourage environmental responsibility and proper waste management among learners.

BodyThe activity was organized by the Science Club with the support of class advisers and student leaders. Participants were assigned to different areas of the campus, including the garden, covered court, classroom corridors, and school entrance.

According to the organizers, the clean-up drive aimed to help students understand the importance of keeping shared spaces safe, clean, and orderly. The activity also reminded learners to segregate waste and reduce the use of single-use plastics.

Quote"We want students to see that caring for the environment begins with small actions they can do every day," said Ana Reyes, Science Club president.

SourceScience Club adviser Mr. Daniel Cruz confirmed that the activity was part of the school's environmental awareness program for the month.

The collected recyclable materials were placed in labeled containers after the activity. Student leaders said they plan to conduct another clean-up drive next month to continue the campaign.

Parts of This Article

Who

More than 120 students, Science Club, advisers, student leaders.

What

Campus clean-up drive.

When

Friday; published July 14, 2026 in this sample.

Where

The Living Stone school grounds.

Why

To promote environmental responsibility and waste management.

How

Students were assigned to campus areas and collected recyclable materials.

Teacher note: Students may use this as a model for structure, but they must write their own article using real details, verified sources, and actual quotes from their event.

Real-Time Online System Architecture

This page explains how Campus Newsroom should work as a full online multi-user platform.

Frontend

Responsive web app for desktop, laptop, tablet, and mobile phones.

Backend API

Handles authentication, articles, comments, evaluations, badges, notifications, and publication.

Database

Stores users, articles, revisions, media, learning data, activity logs, and archives.

Real-Time Layer

WebSocket or Socket.IO broadcasts article updates and notifications to authorized users.

Role Permissions

Student Writer

Creates and edits article content, saves drafts, submits, revises, tracks progress, and earns badges.

Teacher / News Adviser

Reviews student work, adds comments, evaluates writing, and returns or approves articles.

Editor

Reviews approved articles, checks quality, organizes sections, and schedules publication.

Publication Administrator

Manages users, categories, content, final publication, and archives.

Real-Time Workflow

Student submits article

Teacher dashboard updates and teacher receives a notification.

Teacher gives feedback

Student receives notification and article timeline updates.

Editor approves article

Writer sees updated status immediately.

Article is published

It appears on The Living Stone publication page.

Collaboration Safety Rule

Student writers own article body editing. Teachers add comments and evaluations. Editors handle publication review. Administrators manage users, categories, archives, and website content. This prevents accidental overwriting.
